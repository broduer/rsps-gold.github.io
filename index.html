RSPS Gold Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers about current rates, supported servers, payment methods, delivery, account access and how to verify the correct Discord account.

How do I buy RSPS gold?

Send a6d9 the RSPS server name and the amount you want to buy. You will receive a current quote covering the unit rate, available stock, payment options, complete order total and delivery plan before deciding whether to proceed.

How are RSPS gold rates calculated?

Rates depend on the server, amount, current stock, demand and the available transfer method. Confirm both the unit rate and the complete order total before payment.

Which RSPS servers are supported?

Dedicated gold pages are available for Impact, Roat Pkz, SpawnPK, Alora, RuneX, Orion, Ferox and Near-Reality. Other private servers may be considered case by case, depending on current stock and available transfer options.

Is RSPS gold always in stock?

No. Stock varies by server, amount and current availability. Stock is confirmed as part of the quote before payment.

Can I pay with Apple Pay, Visa or Mastercard?

Depending on your location and checkout eligibility, Eldorado.gg checkout may include Apple Pay, Visa, Mastercard, credit cards and debit cards. The final payment methods are shown by Eldorado.gg during checkout.

Can I pay with cryptocurrency or RuneScape gold?

Eligible direct trades may support Bitcoin, Ethereum, Litecoin or Tether. Some orders may also be completed through an agreed OSRS or RS3 gold exchange, depending on the server, amount, stock and current exchange rate.

Does rsps-gold.com process payments?

No. This website provides information and verified contact details for arranging a trade. Payment is completed only through the method agreed during the conversation, such as Eldorado.gg checkout or an eligible direct-payment option.

Do buyers need to share an account password?

For a standard gold purchase, buyers normally should not need to share an account password. Never share email access, recovery information, authenticator codes or payment credentials.

How do I verify the correct A6D9 Discord account?

The Discord username is a6d9 and the numeric user ID is 640265737050652672. Check the user ID before sharing order details or sending payment.

How is RSPS gold delivered?

The available transfer method depends on the private server and is confirmed before payment. Delivery follows the agreed server, currency, quantity and transfer plan.

How long does it take to receive a quote or delivery?

Timing varies depending on availability, server, amount, stock and transfer method. No fixed response or delivery time is guaranteed, so confirm the expected plan before paying.

What should I confirm before payment?

Confirm the server, currency, quantity, unit rate, complete order total, available stock, payment method, delivery method and verified Discord user ID. Do not proceed while any detail remains unclear.
